From product link to parcel

CSSBuy finds and product links

For Taobao clothing links, Weidian sneaker listings and 1688 accessories, open the original listing and confirm the seller, model and variant. Check product availability for your size and read the seller return policy. Keep a copy of the variant name in case the spreadsheet entry changes.

Spreadsheet size and fit guide

Start with a sneaker size chart and compare foot length in centimeters. For clothes, use hoodie chest measurements, T-shirt shoulder width, jacket sleeve length and pants waist measurements from a garment you own. For headwear, compare cap circumference and adjuster range.

CSSBuy QC photos

Quality control photos show what arrived at the warehouse. For shoes, inspect the size label, outsole and left–right symmetry. For jerseys, look at print placement and seams. For accessories, ask for clasp close-ups, hardware details and a measurement photo if the scale is unclear.

CSSBuy shipping cost guide

Build a landed cost estimate from the item price, domestic shipping, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Compare actual parcel weight and volumetric weight when a carrier uses dimensions. Confirm whether parcel consolidation changes the shipping quote.

Delivery by destination

For US delivery, check your ZIP code and the route's tracking coverage. For UK delivery, check the full postcode and how the carrier handles import charges. For EU delivery, compare estimates for your specific country and check how VAT is handled at checkout or delivery.

A shortlist for your first order

Save the marketplace URL, chosen color, size chart, seller notes and return deadline for each item. Check stock before payment, review the warehouse photos when available, and keep the packaging dimensions and carrier quote with your order notes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a curated list of product links used for shopping research. Some sheets also include product photos, category labels or notes. Check the marketplace listing for current details.

What should I check if a product link is broken?

Search for the item title or model code on the original marketplace. Before ordering from a different listing, compare the seller, photos, size chart and available variants.

Can QC photos prove a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you check visible condition and the selected variant. Use an authorized seller or independent authentication when provenance matters.

Does the spreadsheet price include international shipping?

Usually the listed price refers to the item. Ask for a parcel quote based on the destination, packed dimensions, chargeable weight and shipping route.

Where do the category links lead?
